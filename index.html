Head settings
Framing rules
  • Headroom. Keep it tight: just a small gap above the head, with the eyes in the top third of the frame. In a close-up it is fine to trim the top of the head slightly. Too much space above the head makes the subject look like they are sinking.
  • Look room. When someone looks to one side, place them on the opposite third so there is open space in front of their face.
  • Walking room. When the subject moves, keep extra space ahead of them. Lead the move instead of chasing it.
  • Facing the camera. Center them, with the eyes still on the upper third.
  • Cowboy shot. Cut at mid-thigh, never at the knees or the waist. It shows posture and gestures and works well for a pastor who moves around.
  • Full shots. Leave a little room above the head and below the feet.
